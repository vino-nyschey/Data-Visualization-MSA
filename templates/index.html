Protein Species Name

This pie chart visualizes different species names of the proteins. By hovering, you get exact probabilities for the proportion of this species in relation to all 89 proteins. More information about the category 'Other Species' is also included in the hover text.

Protein Family Name

This bar chart shows the protein families with their associated number of proteins. Independent of the other charts, you can select different species and only display them in the plot.
